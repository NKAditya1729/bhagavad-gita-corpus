# Review of the machine-made padapāṭha — by the Aṣṭādhyāyī

*25 October 2026. Scope: every word whose पद form was made by the machine (dotted underline in पद view), in all
18 chapters and all seven texts (Śāṅkarabhāṣya, Ānandagiri, Bhāṣyārkaprakāśa, Bhāṣyotkarṣadīpikā, Śrīdharī,
Nīlakaṇṭhī, Madhusūdanī). Harsha's own splits were not changed, except to keep proper names whole (§4).*

## 1. What was reviewed

100,383 distinct machine splits, occurring 124,792 times. Every one was checked by program, four ways. Each rule is
cited by sūtra; every sūtra number and text was checked against the ashtadhyayi.com data (`tools/verify_sutras.py`).

## 3. What was withdrawn, and why

4,967 words are now shown as printed. Most are **damaged in the source text** (Harsha's OCR), so no correct split exists
until the text itself is corrected: राग्द्वेष (for रागद्वेष), सुघदुःख (सुखदुःख), शुद्य्धार्थ, अप्रस्तत, र्गमनं, देषाम्.
Others are run-together fragments (मित्याद्यारभ्य, म्-इत्यादि) or analyses that need a sūtra the checker does not model
(e.g. अहोरात्र, 8.2.68 with its vārttika). The full list, with the reason for each, is in
[`withdrawn-words.tsv`](withdrawn-words.tsv) — a worklist for the later OCR correction.

## 4. Decisions of method (please confirm)

1. **A samāsa is one pada** (1.2.46 कृत्तद्धितसमासाश्च, 2.4.71 सुपो धातुप्रातिपदिकयोः). In पद view its members are shown
   with hyphens, as Harsha does; a space only ever separates padas.
2. **Proper names and saṃjñās are not broken** — प्रजापति, हिरण्यगर्भ, मधुसूदन, जनार्दन, गुडाकेश, केशिनिषूदन, वृकोदर,
   अनन्तविजय, शङ्कराचार्य … (list in `tools/review.py`). This was also applied to Harsha's own splits (प्रजा-पतेः, 13
   places) and to 8 mūla lines (e.g. 3.10 प्रजा-पतिः → प्रजापतिः, 10.20 गुडाका-ईश → गुडाकेश, 1.15 वृक-उदरः → वृकोदरः).
   Words that are names only sometimes are **not** on the list: ब्रह्मसूत्र (13.4, which Śaṅkara reads as "words
   indicating Brahman"), कामधुक् (3.10 इष्टकामधुक्), सत्यकाम (सत्यकामत्वम्).
3. **नञ् stays with its word** (अशोच्याः, अनुक्तत्वात्), as Harsha does in the mūla (अशोच्यान्, अगत-असून्).
4. A member is shown in its **pada form**: तद्- or तत्- are both pausal forms by 8.4.56 वाऽवसाने (Harsha uses both).

## 5. What a rule check cannot settle

Where two analyses are both grammatical, the rules cannot choose; only the sense can. Example: विषयसंप्रयोगात्मागर्धः
was split …आत्मा अगर्धः, which joins back correctly (6.1.101) but means the opposite of the intended …आत्मा गर्धः
("greed"). In a random hand-check of 90 accepted splits, about 2–3 were of this kind or came from OCR damage.
Such cases need a reader; they are still marked with the dotted underline.

a one-letter piece); a single आ- member was left alone, since it is a member in its own right (2.1.13 आङ् मर्यादाभिविध्योः,
